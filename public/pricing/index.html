Why the free plan has daily limits

Searching is a database query and costs us almost nothing, so its allowance is generous and exists only to deter automated extraction. Call import, document analysis and eligibility assessment each send your documents to a language model, and each one costs real money every time it runs. The allowances are per rolling 24 hours — they reset a day after your first use, not at midnight — and any paid plan removes all of them.

Cancelling

At any time, from Billing in your workspace. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and afterwards the account continues on the free plan: proposals, dossiers and pipeline stay readable and editable.
