Help centre

Answers about discovery, alerts, proposal preparation and accounts.

Where do opportunities come from?

GrantCrafter indexes public information from official donor, government and multilateral sources. Each record links to its original call.

How do alerts work?

Select countries and thematic areas and receive newly indexed, still-open matches daily or weekly.

Can I save opportunities?

Yes. Add a call to Favorites while browsing, then open Favorites from the app navigation.

Does GrantCrafter guarantee funding?

No. It supports research, drafting and compliance; donors make all funding decisions.
